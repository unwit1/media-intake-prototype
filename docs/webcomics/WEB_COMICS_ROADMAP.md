## Book-series stress and usability follow-up

Comics catalog follow-up is implemented separately from web-native page extraction: issue-first multi-provider search, conservative run/edition reconciliation, provenance, credential settings, individual issue watches and shared approved download-source searching. See [Comics metadata coverage and structured backlog](COMICS_METADATA_SEARCH.md) for lazy detailed credits, more pages, Metron/GCD series watchers, identity review and print-collection relationships; these do not replace the Webcomics ingestion priorities above.

Implemented: multi-select search results, a bulk series preview, one background import with per-series outcomes, duplicate reuse, future-date scheduling, bounded search pool and indexed queue lookup. `tests/test_book_batch.py` exercises eight series, 57 distinct books including shared works and eight upcoming books, real local HTTP EPUB transfers, a transient failure, restart and a 2,000-row unrelated queue.

Implemented 2026-10-01: batches and per-series outcomes persist in `book_batches`/`book_batch_items`; pause, resume, cancel (added series stay in Watched) and retry of failed/cancelled series; unfinished batches resume automatically when the app starts; series are claimed atomically with a 15-minute lease, so two processes never process the same series and a crashed claim is retaken; series-level progress aggregates child books as downloaded, upcoming, errored and waiting. The Watched tab's **Book batches** window shows each batch and series with these controls. `tests/test_book_batch.py` covers a 100-series add interrupted after 37 and resumed after a reconnect, crashed and live claims, two concurrent connections, pause from another connection, cancel/retry and progress aggregation.

Implemented 2026-10-02: **Add series from a list…** in Book batches takes up to 100 pasted titles (bullets, numbering and duplicates ignored). Each title is searched like the Books category. A series is added automatically only when its match score is at least 0.92 and at least 0.05 ahead of the next candidate. Uncertain titles keep up to five candidates in a saved draft batch for **Review matches** (use a match, skip, reconsider). Titles with no match or a failed search are reported without blocking the others. A batch with open choices waits as *needs_review*, and choices made after a restart continue the same batch. Tests: `TitleListTests` in `tests/test_book_batch.py`.

Implemented 2026-10-02:
- **Source health.** Every download and source search records an outcome. Queue → **Source health** shows each source's status, success counts, speed and latest error, and automatic downloads try failing sources last (`source_health.py`, `tests/test_source_health.py`).
- **Watched-source uniqueness across processes.** A partial unique index on `watched_items.source_url` covers all new rows. Duplicates that existed before are kept and flagged `legacy_duplicate`, never merged. An insert that loses a race to another app window updates that window's row instead (`tests/test_unique_watch_sources.py`).

All book-series P1 items in this section are now implemented. Acceptance: a 100-series add can resume after restart, uncertain titles remain reviewable, downloaded/future/failed counts are clear, and one bad catalog result does not hide successful adds. Reuse `book_batch.py`, existing metadata search and shared watch/queue infrastructure.
